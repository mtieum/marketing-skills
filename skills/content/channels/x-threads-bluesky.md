# X, Threads, Bluesky

**What works:** a single specific observation or number; short threads (3–6 posts) for a
story. Images help. Replies to people in the niche build more than broadcasting does.

**Item body:**
```
PLATFORMS: x | threads | bluesky
POST 1 (≤ 280 chars for X; 500 Threads; 300 Bluesky)
POST 2…n (if a thread)
IMAGE: file, alt text, credit
LINK: in the last post (links in the first post get less reach on X)
```

**Rules:** no hashtag lists (0–1), no auto-DMs, no follow/unfollow, disclose ads.
