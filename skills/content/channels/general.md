# General rules (any channel without its own playbook)

- One idea per item. Lead with the most specific, useful detail, not the brand.
- Native to the platform: its length, its format, its tone. Read 10 recent top posts in the
  target space before writing the first one, and note what they have in common in `log.md`.
- One clear next step, at the end, if the platform allows links.
- Disclose ads, sponsorships and affiliate links plainly (UK: "Ad" / "#ad"; ASA/CAP code).
- Never: fake accounts, buying engagement, posing as a customer, commenting on our own posts
  from another account, mass tagging, copying others' content, AI pictures of real things.
- Add the channel to `brand.md` and ask for a proper playbook if it's used more than twice.
