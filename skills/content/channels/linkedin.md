# LinkedIn

**What it is:** professional audience; best for B2B (agencies, operators, partners, buyers).
Personal profiles reach far more people than company pages; posts usually go out from the
founder's profile and are shared by the page.

**What works:** a first line that stands alone (only ~2 lines show before "…more"), a short
story or lesson from real work, specific numbers, short paragraphs, a question or plain
next step at the end. Document (PDF carousel) posts and native video do well.

**Item body:**
```
PROFILE: founder | company page
HOOK (first line, ≤ 150 chars)
BODY (≤ 1,300 chars works best; 3,000 max)
LINK: in the first comment, not the post (links in posts get less reach) — write the comment too
FORMAT: text | image (1200×1200 or 1080×1350) | document (PDF pages, 1080×1350) | video
HASHTAGS: 0–3
```

**Rules**
- Name a customer or partner only with their permission; tag people only if they were involved.
- No engagement pods, no "comment YES for the guide" bait.
- Client results need the client's sign-off before posting (note who approved).
