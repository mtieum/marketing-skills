# Campaign

## Steps

1. Read `brand.md`, `plan.md` (this campaign's entry), `log.md`, and the channel playbooks
   for its channels (`skills/content/channels/`).

2. **Brief** (`campaigns/<slug>/brief.md`), one page:
   - **Audience** and the single thing they should do (the win).
   - **Angle**: the one true, specific claim. Then 3–5 supporting points, each with its source.
   - **Hero asset**: the one thing everything else points to (a page, a story, a tool, a video).
     If it doesn't exist, it's the first item.
   - **Per channel**: format, how many, what each piece does differently (not the same text pasted).
   - **Link and tracking**: target URL per channel with UTM tags
     `utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=<social|email|cpc|referral>&utm_campaign=<slug>`.
   - **Success after 3 weeks**: numbers for keep / change / stop.
   - **Risks**: platform rules, claims needing care, anyone it could annoy.

3. **Items.** Add dated rows to the brief's item list (date, channel, kind, producer, status).
   Don't write the items here; the producers do, and they land in `queue/`.

4. Show the owner the angle, hero asset and item count, and flag anything needing a decision.

## A good angle
- True, checkable, and specific to this brand (its data, its product, its way of doing things).
- Useful to the audience even if they never click.
- Survives "so what?" from a busy stranger.
