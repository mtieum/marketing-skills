# Plan

A plan a solo owner can actually keep. Fewer channels done well beat every channel done thinly.

## Inputs
`marketing/brand.md`, `log.md`, the newest `metrics/` reports, the current `plan.md`,
`outreach.csv`, and the brand's available proof and domain producers.

## Steps

1. **Where are we.** One paragraph: what's live, what's been tried (from the log), what the
   numbers say. If this is the first plan, say there's no baseline and the first goal is one.

2. **Goals (2–3).** Each tied to a win in `brand.md` and measurable from a listed data source,
   with a number and a date. e.g. "20 referring domains by 2026-12-20", "300 Pinterest outbound
   clicks a month". If a goal can't be measured with the sources listed, fix the measurement
   first (that becomes a task) or pick another goal.

3. **Campaigns (2–3).** Each one an angle, not a channel: something true and specific the
   brand can say, for one audience, carried across the channels that audience uses. For each:
   name (slug), audience, the angle in one sentence, why now, channels, the producer, the goal
   it serves, and what result after 3 weeks means keep / change / stop.
   Prefer angles built on the brand's own proof (data, tools, real work) over generic topics.

4. **Cadence.** Per channel, from `brand.md` and the channel playbook, sized to ~20 minutes
   of review a week in total. Say what's dropped to make room.

5. **Paid (optional).** At most one paid test running at a time, inside the caps in
   `brand.md`, only for an angle that has already done well organically or to answer a
   specific question.

6. **Calendar.** A dated table for 6 weeks: date, channel, campaign, item, producer.
   Respect events and seasons that matter to the audience.

7. **Write** `plan.md` (replace; the old one goes into `log.md` as a one-paragraph summary),
   create `campaigns/<slug>/brief.md` stubs for new campaigns (the `campaign` skill fills them),
   and show the owner the goals, campaigns and the first two weeks.

## Don't
- Don't add a channel the owner hasn't got an account for without saying it needs setting up.
- Don't plan more than one new channel at a time.
- Don't re-propose anything the log says was stopped, unless the reason has changed (say why).
