# Short video (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)

**What works:** a hook in the first 1–2 seconds that promises something specific, 3–5 quick
beats each showing one real thing, a payoff, ~20–40 s total. Captions burned in (most watch muted).

**Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Text inside the safe area (keep 250 px clear at the bottom and
~150 px at the top and right for the app's buttons).

**Script file** (the item body):
```
HOOK (0–2s): on-screen text / voice-over
BEAT 1: label, visual (file + credit), VO line
BEAT 2: …
PAYOFF + CTA: what to do (follow, save, "link in bio"), not "check out our website"
CAPTION: platform caption (TikTok ≤150 chars best, IG ≤ 2 lines before "more")
HASHTAGS: 3–5 specific (place/topic + niche), no #fyp stuffing
SOUND: licensed track from the platform's commercial library, or none
```

**Rules**
- Links don't work in captions on TikTok/Reels; point to the profile link or a pinned comment.
- Business accounts can only use the platform's commercial music library.
- Footage: own, or licensed for commercial use and edits, credited. Never AI footage of real places.
- One video per idea; repost the same video across the three platforms, but write each caption for its platform.
- One cover everywhere: the frame where the title is on screen. Add the still as an image in the
  item's `assets` (after the video) and its time as `cover_at` (seconds); set the same frame on
  every platform, by hand or through the gateway.
- If the brand has a renderer (see `brand.md` Domain producers), produce its input file too.
