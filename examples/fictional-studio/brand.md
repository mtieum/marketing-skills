# Example Pottery Studio — a fictional example

A made-up brand to show what a filled-in profile looks like. Every name and number here is
invented; a real profile holds only real facts.

## What it is
A small pottery studio running evening wheel classes for beginners and selling its own tableware online.
One line: "Learn to throw a pot in six evenings, then buy the mugs we make."
It is not: a craft-supplies shop, a kiln-hire service.

## Audiences
1. **Local adults wanting a hobby**
   - Where: local Facebook groups, Instagram, Google Maps searches ("pottery class near me").
   - Want: a friendly first class, small groups, everything provided.
   - Stops them: price, worry about being bad at it, evening clashes.
2. **Gift buyers**
   - Where: Pinterest, Instagram, gift guides in local magazines.
   - Want: a handmade present or a class voucher.

## What counts as a win
| Conversion | Where it's measured | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Class booking | booking system export | main goal |
| Voucher sale | shop orders | second |
| Newsletter sign-up | newsletter tool | supporting |

## Voice
- Sounds like: warm, practical, a little self-deprecating about wonky first pots.
- Never: "artisanal journey", "elevate your", stock-photo hands in clay.
- Sample:

> Your first bowl will lean. Ours did too. By week three the walls stand up, and by week six you take home four pieces you made start to finish.

## Proof and assets
Own photos of every class (with students' permission), the glaze recipes the studio developed, class completion counts.
